Water Cat 🐈‍⬛💧

Steps to install and run. Takes about five minutes.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.
  • Apple's free command line tools. To check, open Terminal and run the line below. If they're already installed it will say so. Otherwise a window pops up, so click Install and wait for it to finish. 
    xcode-select --install

Install

  1. Download the code. On the GitHub repo page, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP. Double-click the ZIP in your Downloads folder to unzip it.
  2. Open Terminal in that folder. Type cd (with a space), drag the unzipped folder onto the Terminal window, and press Return. For example: 
    cd ~/Downloads/watercat-main
  3. Build the app. This takes under a minute and ends with Built build/Water Cat.app. 
    bash build.sh
  4. Move it to Applications. 
    cp -R "build/Water Cat.app" /Applications/

Run

  1. Start her. Open Water Cat from Launchpad or Spotlight. A small cat head appears in your menu bar. Nothing else shows up yet. She'll come back in 30 minutes.
  2. See her right away (optional): 
    open "/Applications/Water Cat.app" --args --now
  3. Dismiss her by clicking on her. Your screens stay dimmed and your apps won't respond until you do.
  4. Start her automatically. Click the cat in the menu bar and turn on Open at Login.

The menu bar cat

  • Next sip in … min: when she's due.
  • Remind Me Now: call her up immediately.
  • Pause / Resume Reminders: for meetings and presentations.
  • Open at Login: start her when your Mac starts.
  • Quit Water Cat.

She shows up every 30 minutes. Between 11:30–1:00 and 3:30–5:00 she comes back more often, at random 10–20 minute gaps.

If something goes wrong

Stuck behind the dim screen? Press ⌘ ⌥ Esc, choose Water Cat, and click Force Quit.
  • "command not found: swiftc" when building: the command line tools aren't installed. Run xcode-select --install and try again.
  • Open at Login won't turn on: make sure the app is in /Applications, not still in the build folder.

Uninstall

Turn off Open at Login, choose Quit Water Cat from the menu bar, then drag Water Cat from Applications to the Bin.
